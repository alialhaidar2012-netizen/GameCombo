🏃 Who Are You?

You are a green square.

You must jump over gaps and land on platforms.

🎮 How to Play

Any Key or Tap

Press any key on keyboard.
OR
Tap the screen on a touch screen.

= JUMP

⚠️ Be Careful!

Do NOT touch the side of a platform!

If you touch the side = YOU DIE!

🏆 Goal

Jump from platform to platform.
Go as far as you can!
Reach the Score to win and get trophies!
