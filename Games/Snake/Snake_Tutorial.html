🍎 Points & Winning

Every time you eat a red square (apple), you get 1 point.

You need 10 points to win the game!

Your points are shown at the top of the screen:

📊 Points: X out of 10

Less than 10 points when you die = You lose
10 or more points = You win!

🐍 Who Are You?

You are a green snake.

Eat the red squares (apples) to grow!

🎮 How to Play

W= Up
S= Down
A= Left
D= Right

On a touch screen: use the buttons on screen.

⚠️ Be Careful!

Do NOT hit the walls!

Do NOT hit your own tail!

🏆 Goal

Eat 10 red apples to win!

Grow as long as you can!
