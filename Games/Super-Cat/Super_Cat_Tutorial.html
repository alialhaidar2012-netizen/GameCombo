🐱 Story

You are a brave black cat on a dangerous mission.

The Queen of the Tiger Cats has stolen the sacred diamonds from the people and hidden them deep inside her temple!

In this first level, you must find your way to the entrance of the temple to begin your mission.

But be careful – the Queen's orange guardian cats already patrol the area. They will catch you if you get too close!

⌨️ Keyboard

W = Jump
A = Left
D = Right

📱 Touch

Use the on-screen buttons to move and jump.

W = Jump
A = Left
D = Right

🏆 How to Win

Navigate through the area and reach the temple entrance to begin your quest for the sacred diamonds!

⚠️ Avoid the orange guardian cats – they protect the temple and will catch you!

🕳️ Watch out for the pits – don't fall in!

Make it to the entrance and get ready to enter the temple!
